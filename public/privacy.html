Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This tool is run by Golden Paw Studios. Contact: goldenpaw.dogeadmin@gmail.com.

Your files

Videos and images you open in the tool are processed entirely inside your web browser. They are not uploaded to our servers or to any third party, and we cannot see them. Exported files are created on your device.

Information stored on your device

We store your light/dark theme preference in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this site and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at www.aboutads.info. To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the site is used (pages visited, approximate location, device type, and anonymous events such as "a file was exported"). Google Analytics uses cookies. It never receives the contents of your files.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, advertising and analytics cookies are off by default. We ask for your consent through a Google-certified consent message. You can change your choice at any time using the privacy settings link shown by that message.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Google Firebase Hosting, which may process standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and secure the site.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to processing. Because we do not hold accounts or files, most data relevant to you is held by Google. Use the links above or contact us.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change, and revise the date at the top.
