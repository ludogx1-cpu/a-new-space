Reporting a bug

It helps a lot if you include:

  • Your browser and version (for example, Chrome 131 on Windows 11).
  • The video format and roughly how long the clip is.
  • The settings you used and what went wrong. A screenshot is ideal.

Please don't attach your original video unless we ask for it. The tool never uploads files, and we would rather not receive them either.
