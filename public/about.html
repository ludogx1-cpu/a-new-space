About AI Video Asset Creator

A free browser tool that turns video clips into sprite sheets, GIFs and frame packs for games, apps and the web.

AI video models and screen recorders make it easy to produce animation, but game engines and web pages don't want an MP4. They want a packed sprite sheet, individual transparent frames, and metadata that says where each frame lives. Getting there usually means a chain of desktop tools: a video editor to trim, a keyer to strip the background, a texture packer to build the sheet.

This site does that whole chain in one page. Load a clip, choose the section you want, key out the background, and export a sprite sheet with a TexturePacker-format JSON atlas, a ZIP of frames, or an animated GIF.

Private by design

All decoding, keying and encoding runs in your browser with standard web APIs. Your videos are never uploaded. That makes the tool fast, keeps working on confidential or unreleased footage, and means there is no file-size cap imposed by a server.

How the site is funded

The tool is free with no watermark and no account. It is paid for by advertising shown around the tool (never inside your exports) and by optional support from users who find it useful.
