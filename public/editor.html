Sprite Sheet Editor & Player

Preview, clean up and re-export any sprite sheet. Files stay on your device.

1. Load a sprite sheet

Drag & drop both files onto this panel. With a JSON atlas (TexturePacker JSON Hash or Array, as exported by our converter, TexturePacker, Aseprite, Phaser…) frames are cut exactly, trim offsets included. Without one, slice by grid below.

2. Player

— / —
Shortcuts: Space play/pause · ←/→ step · Del delete selected · Ctrl+A select all · Ctrl+Z undo

3. Edit frames

Click to select, Shift-click for a range, Ctrl/⌘-click to toggle. Drag a frame to reorder. Double-click a frame to show it in the player.

4. Export

Exports a sheet image plus a TexturePacker JSON Hash atlas (works in Phaser, PixiJS and our converter's format). Frames are written at their full, untrimmed size so they line up exactly.

What can the sprite sheet editor do?

It's the clean-up step after you've made a sprite sheet. Load a sheet from our video converter or anywhere else, watch it animate at any frame rate, and fix it: cut the frames where the loop stutters, drop blurry in-betweens, reorder or reverse a sequence, duplicate a held pose, or split one long sheet into separate animations by exporting just the frames you select.

Tips

  • Finding a seamless loop: step through with the arrow keys and delete the frame that duplicates frame 1. That removes the hitch at the loop point.
  • Splitting animations: select the idle frames, export them as idle, then select the run frames and export them as run.
  • No JSON? Count the columns and rows in the sheet and use Slice by grid. Skip empty cells drops blank cells at the end of the last row.
