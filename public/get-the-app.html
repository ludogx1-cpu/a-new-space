Get the app

Install the Golden Paw Animation Creation Tool on your phone, tablet or computer. It opens in its own window, works offline, and keeps every file on your device.

💻 Windows, Mac, Linux & ChromeOS (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open the Animation Creation Tool in Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (a monitor with a down arrow), or open the menu ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app (in Edge: … → Apps → Install this site as an app).
  3. Click Install. The app appears in your Start menu, Dock or app launcher.

🤖 Android

  1. Open the site in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app when prompted, or tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen → Install.

🍎 iPhone & iPad

  1. Open the site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.

Note: iOS limits what web apps can decode. If a video won't load, convert it to MP4 (H.264) first.

🧩 Browser extension (Chrome & Edge)

The extension puts a 🐾 button in your toolbar that opens the converter and Sprite Editor instantly, fully offline, with no ads.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons. Until then, it can be installed manually: unzip the extension package, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode and click Load unpacked.

Is it safe?

Yes. The app and extension are the same tool as this website. Videos and images are processed on your device and are never uploaded. See our privacy policy.
